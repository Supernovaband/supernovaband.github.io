The band

FOUR PEOPLE.
ONE SOUND.

Supernova is an emerging rock band based in Fredrikstad, Norway, operating out of the St. Croix-huset music community.

The four-piece lineup delivers a high-energy sound that blends classic rock foundations with modern alternative influences.

Known locally for their dynamic stage presence at the Husrock concert series, Supernova is quickly establishing a reputation as one of the region's promising young live acts.
